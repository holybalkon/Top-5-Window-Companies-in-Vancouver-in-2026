About This Vancouver Window Companies Guide

Updated November 25, 2026

This page explains how the Top 5 Window Companies in Vancouver guide is put together, what we rely on, and who stands behind it.

Publisher disclosure

This guide is a curated editorial comparison published by independent window industry expert Daniel Marsh. Canadian Choice Windows & Doors is listed first as the publisher's recommendation.

The written review on the home page is the publisher's own expert opinion.

Methodology

  1. Select established window replacement providers that serve Vancouver and Metro Vancouver.
  2. Read each company's official pages for products, installation approach, warranty and service area.
  3. Summarize strengths and points to verify, using the same structure for every company.
  4. Mark claims taken from a company's own website with a company-stated tag.
  5. Include prices only when a company publishes them, and label them as company-stated.

What this guide does not do

  • It does not claim hands-on testing of any product or installation.
  • It does not publish invented ratings, review counts, quotes or competitor shortcomings.
  • It does not set a universal price per window. Any price shown is a company-stated range, and the final price comes from a written quote.
  • Warranty statements are summaries of company claims. The contract, product and exclusions control.
