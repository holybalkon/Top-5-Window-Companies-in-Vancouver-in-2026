How to choose a window company in Vancouver

Vancouver's rainy climate makes water management and installation methodology especially important. Before choosing a contractor, compare proposals on the same number of windows, opening styles, frame material and glass specification. Ask whether installation includes removal, disposal, waterproofing, interior finish work and final cleanup.

What to verify when comparing window companies
FactorWhat to verify
Window performanceENERGY STAR listing (where applicable), U-factor, energy rating, glazing, acoustic performance
Installation scopeRetrofit vs full-frame; flashings, sill pans, sealants, rot repair and finishing
WarrantyGlass, hardware, frame, seals, labour, exclusions and transfer provisions
PriceItemized scope, taxes, permits if needed, payment milestones and change orders
ReviewsMost recent local Google/other independent reviews, response to complaints, project photos
TimelinesManufacturing lead time, installation days and repair/replacement process

Questions to ask before signing

  • Which exact manufacturer, model and glazing configuration will be installed?
  • Is each window ENERGY STAR certified for the offered configuration?
  • Will you replace the full frame or install a retrofit insert? Why?
  • How are water intrusion and hidden wood damage handled?
  • Are installers employees or subcontractors? Who responds to warranty calls?
  • What are the exclusions, transfer rules and labour coverage?
  • Does the price cover disposal, interior/exterior trim, cleanup and taxes?

How much does window replacement cost in Vancouver?

There is no reliable universal installed price per window. Costs depend on size, material, glass package, window style, access, moisture damage, trim work and whether the contractor performs a retrofit or a full-frame replacement. A bay window, large custom unit or challenging second-storey opening will generally require a different scope from a standard vinyl slider.

As a reference point, Canadian Choice Windows & Doors states that a standard window in Vancouver typically costs $700 to $1,100 installed, including removal of the old window, trim and disposal company-stated. The same company lists full-home projects from about $7,500 for a small two-bedroom home (15 to 19 windows) up to $60,000 for a luxury estate (41 to 60 windows). Bay, bow, architectural and TrueCrankless windows cost more. See the Canadian Choice Vancouver pricing page. These are one company's own figures, so treat them as a guide rather than a guarantee.

For a fair comparison, request at least three itemized quotations covering the same specifications and work.
